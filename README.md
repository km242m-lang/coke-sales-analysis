# Coca-Cola Retail Sales and Profitability Analysis (2021)

## Overview
Analysis of 2021 Coca-Cola sales across key American retailers, built in Excel/Google Sheets using pivot tables, charts and a slicer.

Business question: How can Coca-Cola optimize sales and profitability across U.S. retailers?

## Dataset
- Dimensions: Retailer, Retailer ID, Invoice Date, Region, State, City, Beverage Brand
- Measures: Price per Unit, Units Sold, Total Sales, Operating Profit, Operating Margin, Month
- Retailers: Sodapop, FizzySip, BevCo, DreamCo
- Regions: Midwest, Northeast, South, Southeast, West
- Brands: Coca-Cola, Diet Coke, Sprite, Fanta, Powerade, Dasani Water

## Workbook Structure
- Data: raw dataset
- Beginner: cleaning checks, sales by month, region, retailer, brand
- Intermediate: sales by brand and month, brand and region (slicer on Beverage Brand)
- Advanced: units sold by retailer

## Key Findings
- Total 2021 sales: $8,684,028
- Peaks: July ($1,041,438) and December ($1,045,775); weakest period is January to April
- Top region: West ($2,835,100); lowest: Midwest ($1,150,110)
- Top retailer: Sodapop ($4,403,630); lowest: DreamCo ($404,413)
- Top brand: Coca-Cola ($2,015,890); lowest: Fanta ($1,031,650)

## Recommendations
1. Protect the West and focus growth on the Midwest
2. Reduce dependence on Sodapop
3. Plan around monthly sales peaks and boost early-year sales
4. Review margin along with sales
